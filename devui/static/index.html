What this shows

A question travels the five stages above. Each one reports what it did, and clicking a stage opens its full output.

ok
Stage ran on real data.
degraded
Stage ran, but something under it is still a placeholder — the model, or a tool that core/ has not implemented.
failed
The verifier found a claim the tool trace does not support. The answer is withheld.

Tier 2 and 3 questions will show degraded until the legality engine lands. That is deliberate: an unbuilt tool errors rather than returning a plausible number.
